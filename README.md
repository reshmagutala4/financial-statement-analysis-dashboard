# Apple Financial Statement Analysis and Ratio Dashboard (2019-2023)

Five years of Apple's financial statements turned into 5 ratios in Excel, with an interactive Power BI dashboard covering profitability, liquidity and leverage.

## Ratios

| Year | Net margin | ROE | ROA | Current ratio | Liabilities / equity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2019 | 21.2% | 61.1% | 16.3% | 1.54 | 2.74 |
| 2020 | 20.9% | 87.9% | 17.7% | 1.36 | 3.96 |
| 2021 | 25.9% | 150.1% | 27.0% | 1.07 | 4.56 |
| 2022 | 25.3% | 197.0% | 28.3% | 0.88 | 5.96 |
| 2023 | 25.3% | 156.1% | 27.5% | 0.99 | 4.67 |

Formulas: net margin = net income / revenue, ROE = net income / total equity, ROA = net income / total assets, current ratio = current assets / current liabilities, liabilities-to-equity = total liabilities / total equity.

## What the numbers show

- **Profitability:** net margin rose from 21.2% to 25.3%, and ROA rose from 16.3% to 27.5%
- **Liquidity:** the current ratio fell from 1.54 to 0.99, and was below 1 in 2022
- **Leverage:** liabilities-to-equity rose from 2.74 to 4.67 (peak 5.96 in 2022)
- ROE is very high because equity fell from $90.5B (2019) to $62.1B (2023) while net income rose

## Dashboard

Three charts: profitability (net margin, ROE, ROA), liquidity (current ratio) and leverage (liabilities-to-equity), by year.

## Notes

- Figures are from Apple's annual financial statements, fiscal years 2019 to 2023
